📊 Understanding Z-Scores

Positive Z-Score: Trending Market (Higher = More Trending)
Negative Z-Score: Mean Reverting Market (Lower = More Mean Reverting)
Near Zero: Neutral/Transitional Market

MR (Market Regime): Z-Score from 12 indicators (ADX, ADF, KPSS, ATR, GARCH, Half-Life, Wavelet, PMC, Choppiness, Hurst, PP, Yang). Positive = Trending, Negative = Mean Reverting.
AS (Avg Score): Z-Score from 22 indicators (RSI, Smooth RSI, ROC, ATR, Stochastic, EMA Score, Main Ratio, Trend Score, OBV, BB, AD, Chaikin, Omega, Sortino, Sharpe, VR, WPR, PVT, ADR, EFI, BB ForLoop, Stoch ForLoop). Positive = Uptrend, Negative = Downtrend.
